Detailed Breakdown

All 27 Additional Features

Each feature was delivered on top of the original agreed scope.

1
ALF — AI Staff Assistant
An AI assistant built into the system that staff can chat with to search for operatives, create allocations, run campaigns, and send messages — all from one conversation.
Agreed: Only Sophie (for recruitment). No AI for staff.
2
Microsoft Outlook Email Integration
Connects to Aztec's Outlook account. Incoming emails from operatives automatically appear in B.O.S. alongside WhatsApp and SMS. Includes editable email templates and a unified inbox view.
Agreed: WhatsApp only. Email not mentioned.
3
SMS Channel
Send and receive SMS for operatives who don't have WhatsApp. Appears in the same unified inbox.
Agreed: WhatsApp only.
4
AI Document Verification
AI reads uploaded passports, driving licences, and CSCS cards. Extracts name, date of birth, expiry, and card details automatically. Flags mismatches against the operative's record.
Agreed: Manual document checking only.
5
CV Parsing
Upload a CV and the system reads it, pulls out work history, trade, and an estimated day rate. Staff just confirm or adjust.
Agreed: Not in the original scope.
6
AI Recruitment Adverts
AI generates ready-to-post job adverts for Facebook, LinkedIn, and Indeed — headline, body copy, and hashtags.
Agreed: Adverts included, but no AI generation.
7
Multi-Language Translation
Messages from operatives in 11 languages are automatically translated to English. Staff replies are translated back into the operative's language.
Agreed: Multi-language for Sophie only (4 languages).
8
Upskilling Analytics
Full dashboard tracking every grade change: who was upskilled, by which director, at which site, the cost impact, and approval status. Includes trend charts, leaderboards, and CSV export.
Agreed: "No visibility on skills gaps" was a stated problem. No solution was scoped.
9
Pay Grade & Rate Management
9 billing grades with automatic rate enforcement. 28% minimum margin protection. When a grade rate changes, every operative on that grade updates automatically.
Agreed: "Rate matrices" mentioned as a concept only.
10
Automated Campaigns
Bulk message campaigns to chase documents, complete profiles, collect missing data, or send job offers — with automatic follow-ups and progress tracking per operative.
Agreed: Individual WhatsApp messages only.
11
Data Quality Tracking
Every operative scored on completeness (25 fields). Colour-coded badges show gaps at a glance. One-click button sends a personalised link to the operative asking for the missing info.
Agreed: No data quality tracking.
12
Self-Service Document Portal
Operatives receive a link on their phone, upload their ID and CSCS card without needing a login. AI verifies the documents inline. Works in multiple languages.
Agreed: Staff-managed uploads only.
13
Bulk Import
Upload a spreadsheet and import hundreds of operatives at once. The system validates every row, catches duplicates, and shows a preview before committing.
Agreed: Manual entry only.
14
Smart WhatsApp Management
Handles WhatsApp's 24-hour messaging rules automatically. If the window is closed, the message is queued and delivered as soon as the operative replies. Staff can take over manually at any time.
Agreed: Basic send and receive only.
15
Notifications & To-Do System
Real-time notification bell, activity feed, and a to-do list that surfaces pending document reviews, unread messages, open NCRs, and missing pay rates — all actionable from one place.
Agreed: Document expiry alerts only.
16
Privacy Mode
One toggle hides all pay rates, charge rates, and margins across the entire system. Useful for screen-sharing or when others are looking over your shoulder.
Agreed: Not in scope.
17
Audit Trail
A dedicated page showing every change made in the system — who changed what, when, and what the old and new values were.
Agreed: Mentioned in one line. Built as a full standalone module.
18
Timesheets & PDF Export
Weekly timesheets with approval workflow and professional PDF download.
Agreed: Not in the original scope.
19
Reports Module
Five report sections (Compliance, Labour, NCR, RAP, Timesheets) with date filters and CSV export.
Agreed: Only a basic dashboard.
20
Key Person Tracking
Flag important operatives with a reason (skills, location, leadership, etc.) and keep a full history of who flagged them and why.
Agreed: Not in scope.
21
Onboarding Checklist
Track each operative through four onboarding steps: blue sticker, buddy allocation, 2-week review, and induction completion.
Agreed: A single induction form only.
22
Donseed Attendance Integration
Connects to Donseed biometric scanners. Automatically creates shift records and timesheets from check-in/check-out data. Built and ready — awaiting API access.
Agreed: WhatsApp-based arrival confirmation only.
23
Real-Time Updates
Every change across the system appears live — no page refresh needed. Covers the dashboard, communications, notifications, and all data tables.
Agreed: Not scoped as a feature.
24
Short Links
Clean, short URLs for document upload links and job offers sent via WhatsApp.
Agreed: Not in scope.
25
Location Mapping
Postcodes automatically converted to map coordinates for operatives and sites, ready for travel distance calculations.
Agreed: Postcodes stored as text only.
26
Multi-Scheme Certifications
CPCS, PAL/IPAF, CISRS, and NRSWA cards tracked individually with card numbers, types, and expiry dates.
Agreed: CSCS only.
27
Admin Data Tools
Secure tools for bulk data export and management, with safety checks before any destructive actions.
Agreed: Not in scope.
